Privacy Policy

DailyFoodFighter (referred to as "the Site") prioritizes user privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of data collected when accessing the Site and the governance measures applied.

1. Information Collection & Usage

The Site does not maintain user account registration or process sensitive personal identification numbers. Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and analytic telemetry (such as IP addresses, browser specifications, operating systems, and page request logs) may be automatically cataloged for server optimization and statistical analysis.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

The Site may utilize "cookies"—small text files deposited onto client hardware—to enhance user experience and facilitate relevant contextual advertising.

  • Managing Cookies: Users retain full authority to configure web browser security settings to decline or selectively accept cookies. Disabling cookies may moderately affect interface rendering.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

The Site participates in or intends to integrate third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

  • Google and third-party vendors utilize cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve advertisements calibrated against past user interactions on this Site and across the broader web.
  • Users can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings page.

4. Inquiries & Data Administration

For data privacy queries or editorial communication, please submit via our Online Contact Desk.
