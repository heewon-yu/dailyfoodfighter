Privacy Policy

DailyFoodFighter (referred to as "the Site") prioritizes user privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of data collected when accessing the Site and the governance measures applied.

1. Information Collection & Usage

The Site does not maintain user account registration or process sensitive personal identification numbers. Diagnostic and analytics data (such as IP addresses, browser specifications, operating systems, and page request logs) may be automatically cataloged for server optimization and statistical analysis.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

The Site may utilize "cookies"—small text files deposited onto client hardware—to enhance user experience and facilitate relevant contextual advertising.

  • Managing Cookies: Users retain full authority to configure web browser security settings to decline or selectively accept cookies. Disabling cookies may moderately affect interface rendering.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

The shared site layout includes a Google AdSense script. Its inclusion does not establish ad approval or actual ad delivery. Google and advertising partners may process information when providing their services.

  • Google and third-party vendors utilize cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve advertisements calibrated against past user interactions on this Site and across the broader web.
  • Users can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings page.

Analytics, hosting and storage

The shared layout uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page use and visits. Google services may receive or process the visited URL, IP address, browser information, cookies and other identifiers. Advertising providers may place and read cookies or use web beacons and IP addresses. The scope depends on the service visited and provider settings.

How Google uses partner-site information · Google Privacy Policy · Ad settings · Analytics opt-out browser add-on

Cloudflare Pages and Workers provide hosting. Connection and security data may be processed under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Some game ranking features send records to the server; browser-local settings and records are separate. Browser data can be removed using the service controls or browser site-data settings.

4. Inquiries & Data Administration

Reports are submitted as public GitHub issues and retained according to GitHub and repository policies. Do not include personal or sensitive data in an issue. This site does not provide a separate contact form.

For data privacy queries or editorial communication, use our Online Contact Desk.
